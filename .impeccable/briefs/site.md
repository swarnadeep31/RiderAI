# Surface brief: TrailCast website (all surfaces)

Scope: home (Persuade), trail page and editor (Operate), sign-up, log-in, tour and welcome (Persuade-light), add-a-trail and the AI route builder (Operate). Visitor mode is per surface, as listed.

Keep: the day/night switch (follows the device until chosen), the riding-bike-and-pins hero animation (redrawn in this world), the onboarding tour (its steps and behaviour).

## Direction contract

seed: dealt by dice from seven worlds (truck art, HRTC route board, Survey of India toposheet); user chose the toposheet, 2026-10-26. (Impeccable's concept-seed launcher not installed; dice rolled locally.)

THESIS. Every trail is a map sheet. TrailCast prints the ride the way the Survey of India prints a valley: the route in road red, heights in contour brown, the rider's moment in water blue, and a margin that says what is on the sheet.

FIRST VIEWPORT. A sheet of the Manali to Rohtang valley drawn in contours, the Beas in blue, the road in red; the bike rides it and pins drop as it passes. The sheet's title is the headline; its scale bar is a video timeline you can drag.

VISITOR PATH. Read the sheet title, drag the scale bar, open a ride from the sheet index, follow it on its own sheet, take the sheet away (GPX, KML, trip sheet).

SIGNATURE. Scale bar equals timeline: dragging time moves the rider on the map, on every surface that has a ride.

CROSS-SURFACE. Trail page = the sheet with its neatline, legend (fuel, mechanics, medical, ATMs as map symbols), cross-section (elevation) and gazetteer (key points). Forms are survey records. The tour is a set of sheets.

RISK. Density and hairlines on phones; a cream-paper, serif-label rendition (rejected: colour fields lead, sans lettering).
